AESM Social Sentiment Research — proposed internal research application

DRAFT — NOT EFFECTIVE. This page is a proposed compliance document. No YouTube API client, real comments or approved derived metrics are currently operating.
YSE Finance Privacy Policy

1. Scope and status

The AESM Social Sentiment Research project is an experimental YSE Finance research prototype. It currently uses fictional test examples, does not access YouTube API Services, and has not been authorised to conduct live YouTube-derived sentiment analysis. This document is a proposed set of terms, not an announcement of an operating or approved YouTube-connected service.

2. Research limitations

Any future published analysis, if separately approved, would describe a selected sample of online discussion, not a representative survey of the Australian population, a national consumer-confidence index, a prediction of economic conditions, investment advice, or tax advice. Source coverage, sample sizes, methods and limitations must be disclosed.

3. YouTube API Services — prospective use

If a YouTube-connected feature is approved and introduced, that API Client will use YouTube API Services subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies. By using that YouTube-connected API Client, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. No such API Client is presently provided on this website.

4. Data handling and privacy

Future access to comment text or related metadata depends on separate YouTube approval, platform rules and privacy review. No individual profiling, account linking, sensitive-trait inference or resale of identifiable comment information is planned. The applicable privacy notice must be prominently available and accepted where required before any API Client feature is accessible. This prototype currently holds no live YouTube comments.

5. Retention and deletion

Any future live collection must implement data refresh, retention, revocation and deletion controls consistent with the specific approved scope and YouTube API requirements before processing begins. Extended retention of derived metrics is not assumed unless YouTube accepts the proposed use case under its additional policies. Requests may be directed through the privacy contact identified in YSE Finance's Privacy Policy.

6. Access, intellectual property and attribution

These proposed terms do not grant rights to download, reproduce, republish or train models on YouTube content, nor to bypass YouTube API limits. Future research must identify independently generated metrics as such and must not imply endorsement, certification or sponsorship by YouTube, Google or Australian statistical agencies.

7. Applicable documents and contact

Consult the linked YouTube Terms of Service, YouTube API Developer Policies, Google Privacy Policy and the YSE Finance Privacy Policy. Questions about this proposed research can be directed to the contact method published in the YSE Finance Privacy Policy. Additional enforceable terms may be required after review of the eventual live functionality.
