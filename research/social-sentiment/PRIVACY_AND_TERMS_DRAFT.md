## Proposed YouTube API privacy supplement (not yet effective)

> ### YouTube API Services — proposed research disclosure
>
> **Status:** Proposed. The AESM Social Sentiment Research prototype currently operates with invented, synthetic examples only; **it does not collect YouTube comments or access YouTube user accounts**. This section must not be published as an active data-processing claim until the actual service, rights and governance controls are confirmed.
>
> **Purpose:** Subject to receiving applicable YouTube permission, YSE Finance proposes to use the official YouTube Data API to study publicly visible discussion topics in a limited, pre-selected sample of Australia-relevant economic news videos. This is for independent research into trends in discussion of cost of living, housing, employment, consumer spending and small-business conditions. We do not intend to identify individual users, assess protected traits, link identities across platforms, or offer a nationwide consumer sentiment index.
>
> **Source and service:** Some proposed research inputs would come from the **YouTube API Services**. Users of such future functionality would be subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Any YouTube branding must follow its current [Developer Branding Guidelines](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/branding-guidelines); the present internal synthetic prototype does not embed a YouTube player or display real YouTube data.
>
> **Information categories:** If authorized, processing would be strictly limited to the permitted comment text, publication timing and pre-approved video/topic context that are necessary for aggregate research. Author names, profile images, user/channel identifiers, email addresses, IP addresses, precise location and user-level trajectories will not be deliberately retained for economic research. Public availability alone does not establish unrestricted rights.
>
> **Use and sharing:** Approved discussion data would be used only for permitted economic-research categorization and human methodology validation. No individual profiling or targeting. We would not use raw comment text to train external AI models or transmit it to third-party LLM providers without a separate legal, platform and privacy assessment and any required consent.
>
> **Storage / deletion:** Actual retention must match the specific terms and any written permissions granted. Raw non-authorized YouTube API Data is subject to YouTube's general refresh/delete restrictions (typically 30 calendar days, with earlier deletion where required). Special retention of approved derived/statistical data is **not assumed** without YouTube acceptance of the applicable amendment and exact use case. A documented review, deletion-request, refresh and retraction mechanism would be established before processing real data.
>
> **User rights / contact:** Requests concerning personal data handling, access, deletion, revocation or complaints may be sent through YSE Finance's published privacy contact on [our Privacy Policy](https://ysefinance.com.au/privacy.html), subject to the applicable laws and YouTube terms. YSE Finance would assess any deletion request and act within applicable deadlines. We do not presently offer user-linked YouTube accounts or OAuth authorization.
>
> **Representativeness:** Selected public discussions are not a representative sample of Australians. Any future aggregates would identify sampling methods, dates, limitations and their non-YouTube provenance.
>
> **Changes to this supplement:** The effective publication date and policy version must be supplied only when approved by the owner, reviewed against the final design, and actually deployed.

## Proposed Terms of Use supplement (not effective)

> ### AESM Social Sentiment Research — proposed terms
>
> The AESM social-discussion module is an experimental research product of YSE Finance and is separate from tax agent services and statutory reporting. Any future figures summarize a **selected, non-representative sample** of discussions; they are not a survey of Australian adults, an economic forecast, regulated financial advice, or a composite economic risk index.
>
> For functionality using YouTube API Services, you acknowledge the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), subject to YouTube's applicable developer requirements. YouTube API-derived content and statistics must not be presented as Google-/YouTube-approved economic facts or endorsements. Relevant YouTube branding and links must be included in any actual authorized API-facing interface.
>
> No account access, commercial data resale, individual tracking, demographic inference or unapproved reuse of YouTube API Data is permitted. YSE Finance may withhold or retract a research result where source rights, provider requirements, data accuracy or privacy obligations are not met.
>
> The project is not operational and has not received YouTube approval. These draft terms do not constitute notice for active data collection.
