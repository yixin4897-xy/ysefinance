Sources & calculation

Real income per person = gross disposable income × HFCE volume ÷ HFCE current prices ÷ average population, with unit conversion. Quarterly growth compares consecutive quarters; annual growth compares the same quarter one year earlier.

Population is an average of adjacent quarter-end estimates, not an observed quarterly mean. Income and HFCE inputs are seasonally adjusted; population is original. This calculation has not been independently seasonally adjusted.
